# btrd

Welcome to the official page of **btrd** — a smart automation and performance boost tool for gamers.

## About Us

btrd is a lightweight utility designed to optimize Windows, automate routine tasks, and maximize your PC's performance during gaming sessions. Our program runs quietly in the background without draining your system resources.

## What We Offer

* **Maximum FPS:** Automatically prioritizes game processes and frees up RAM.
* **Smart Macros & Binds:** Create custom commands and rapid clickers for MMOs, shooters, and strategies.
* **Anti-Distraction Mode:** Blocks pop-up notifications and accidental Win-key presses.
* **Quick Launch Profiles:** Launch games alongside Discord, Spotify, and trackers with a single button.
